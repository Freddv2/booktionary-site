Booktionary

Instant definitions for paper books. Entirely offline.

How it works

Hold the phone about 15 cm above the page and put the crosshair on a word. It turns green when the word is recognised; hold steady for about a second and the definition appears. English and French, both directions, no network required.

Tips

  • Good light helps more than anything else.
  • If a "Closer" hint appears, move the phone nearer the page.
  • A slight tilt is fine. The app reads text at a natural reading angle.
  • Tap outside the definition to return to the camera.
