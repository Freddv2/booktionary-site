How to test Booktionary. The app defines words in printed books by camera. It needs real text in front of it, so please point the phone at this page, displayed on any screen at full size, or print it.

  1. Open Booktionary. The opening screen asks which language you are reading in. This matters: the app looks words up in that language ONLY, so a French word in English mode correctly reports "no definition found". Leave it on English for the English lines below, or choose Français for the French ones. You can switch at any time with the EN/FR pill at the top-right of the camera screen.
  2. Press Start reading.
  3. Allow camera access.
  4. Hold the phone roughly 15 cm (6 inches) from one of the large lines below. If nothing is recognised, move a little nearer.
  5. Put the small green ring on a word. A green highlight starts sweeping across that word.
  6. Hold steady for about one second. When the highlight finishes crossing the word, the definition appears.
  7. Swipe left to return to the camera.

Try running (English, resolves to "run") with the language set to English, or mangeaient (French, resolves to the verb "manger") with it set to Français. Pointing the camera at a desk or a blank surface correctly shows only the small green ring at the centre of the screen, because there is no word to read — that is expected, not a failure. The ring never moves and never changes colour: it marks where the app is sampling.

English — set the app to English

The mice were running here.

A luminous evening settled slowly.

Français — set the app to Français

Les enfants mangeaient du pain.

Une chance inouïe les attendait.
