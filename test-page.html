How to test Booktionary. The app defines words in printed books by camera. It needs real text in front of it, so please point the phone at this page, displayed on any screen at full size, or print it.

  1. Open Booktionary and allow camera access.
  2. Hold the phone roughly 15 cm (6 inches) from one of the large lines below. If nothing is recognised, move a little nearer.
  3. Put the crosshair on a word. It turns green and a green outline appears around the word.
  4. Hold steady for about one second. The definition appears.
  5. Tap outside the panel to return to the camera.

Try mangeaient (French, resolves to the verb "manger") or running (English, resolves to "run"). Pointing the camera at a desk or a blank surface will correctly show a red crosshair, because there is no word to read.

Les enfants mangeaient du pain.

The mice were running here.

Une chance inouïe les attendait.

A luminous evening settled slowly.

sous les yeux de leur mère
