Booktionary Android privacy policy

Last updated 8 October 2026. This policy applies to the Android version of Booktionary.

Booktionary is a camera dictionary for printed books. There are no Booktionary accounts, advertisements or advertising trackers. The Android app uses Google components that collect technical diagnostics and usage information, as described below.

Camera images and text

Camera permission allows the app to recognize words on a page. Text recognition runs on the device. Booktionary uses frames in memory for scanning and the visible definition panel; it does not save them to your photo library. Booktionary's dictionary lookup and language-download code does not upload camera images or recognized words.

Packaged Tesseract models provide on-device text recognition for Russian, Ukrainian and Thai.

On compatible devices, a packaged BGE multilingual ranker running through ONNX Runtime processes surrounding sentence text on the device to select a dictionary meaning. The dictionary definitions and equivalents come from local packs. Booktionary does not send this inference to a network model. The inference model is delivered with the app through Google Play and copied into the app's private storage for local use. It can require additional storage and memory.

Google components

Google ML Kit provides on-device text recognition for Latin, Chinese, Japanese and Korean scripts. Google's disclosure lists device and app information, installation or other identifiers, performance metrics, feature configuration and event/error information used for diagnostics and usage analytics. Google states that this diagnostic data is encrypted in transit. These technical reports are distinct from the camera images and words processed locally.

Read Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy for details of Google's processing and retention. Clearing Booktionary's local data does not erase data already handled by Google.

Language downloads and hosting

English definitions are included with the app. Additional definition packs and optional equivalents for English books download separately. The app requests the language catalog and selected packs from booktionary.io. These requests do not include camera images or lookup words. After installation, dictionary lookups and equivalents work offline.

The current catalog and pack host is GitHub Pages. Serving these requests exposes ordinary connection information, including your IP address, to the hosting provider. GitHub states that Pages logs visitor IP addresses for security. Booktionary has not established a retention period for those provider logs. See GitHub's Pages documentation and GitHub's privacy statement.

Local settings and removal

Booktionary stores your language choices, installed dictionary packs, the local inference model and its runtime cache, download state and version-specific review eligibility counters on the device. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app. You can remove local settings and downloads by clearing Booktionary's app storage in Android Settings or uninstalling it. This does not delete hosting or Google records already processed by those providers.

Optional Google Play reviews

If you choose to submit a rating or review through Google's review dialog, Google Play processes that rating and review text. Production reviews can be public; closed-test feedback is private to the developer. You can delete reviews through your Google Play or Google Account. See Google's in-app review disclosure.
