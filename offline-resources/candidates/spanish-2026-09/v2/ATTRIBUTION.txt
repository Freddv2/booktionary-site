Booktionary Spanish translation-pack pilot

These four candidate packs are transformed extracts of Wiktionary translation
content. Each catalog entry identifies the source editions, source snapshots,
and processing dates used for that direction. Source material is attributed to
Wiktionary contributors:

  https://www.wiktionary.org/
  https://en.wiktionary.org/
  https://es.wiktionary.org/
  https://fr.wiktionary.org/

This pilot is distributed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike
4.0 International license (CC BY-SA 4.0):

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Wiktionary text is also available under the GNU Free Documentation License
under Wiktionary's dual-licensing terms. Booktionary distributes these adapted
translation extracts under CC BY-SA 4.0. Changes include filtering source
entries for the requested language direction, extracting translation data,
normalizing source keys, combining edition-specific extracts where listed in
the catalog, and converting the selected data to Booktionary's SQLite format.

These are test candidates. Row availability does not establish that a
translation is correct for a particular sense or context. They have not had
independent Spanish-language review and are not release-approved.
