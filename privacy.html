Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 July 2026

Booktionary collects no data. It contains no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and no accounts.

Camera

The app needs camera access to read words on the page. Frames are processed on your device, in memory, to recognise text and are discarded immediately. No image is ever saved to disk, and no image or text leaves your device.

Network

The app makes no network requests. Dictionaries are bundled inside the app and every lookup happens on the device, which is why it works with no signal.

Data sharing

There is nothing to share. No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted to the developer or to anyone else.

Children

Because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of any age in this respect.

Changes

If a future version collects anything, this policy will be updated before that version ships.
