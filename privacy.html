Booktionary iPhone privacy policy

Last updated 28 October 2026. This policy applies to the iPhone version of Booktionary.

Booktionary has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app tracking. English definitions are included in the app. Additional book dictionaries and word translation packs are optional downloads.

Camera

The app needs camera access to read words on the page. Camera frames are processed on your device to recognise text. The app does not upload camera images or the words you look up.

Network

The app contacts booktionary.io to load the language-pack catalog. If you tap Get for a language or translation, it downloads the selected pack. An internet connection is required for the catalog and downloads. After installation, dictionary lookups and translations run on the device and work offline.

Starting with version 1.0.11, optional translated definitions for English books use Apple's on-device translation models. Downloading the required models needs an internet connection. Once installed, definition translation runs on your device.

Hosting logs

The catalog and pack files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub says Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub's Pages documentation does not specify how long those IP logs are retained. Booktionary does not receive your lookup words or camera images.

App Store privacy label

The App Store lists “Device ID” under “Data Linked to You” for “App Functionality”. This disclosure covers the IP addresses that our hosting provider retains for security when serving the catalog and downloads. Apple directs developers to classify IP addresses according to their use; we disclose this hosting information conservatively as an identifier linked to a device. It is not used by Booktionary for advertising or cross-app tracking. The app does not request an advertising identifier or send a hardware identifier, camera images or lookup history to the catalog host.

See Apple’s app privacy guidance and GitHub’s Pages documentation. The Android privacy policy separately describes the Google components used by the Android app.

Settings

Your language choices and downloaded packs are stored on your device. They are not sent to Booktionary.

Data sharing

Booktionary does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The hosting provider receives the network information needed to serve catalog and pack requests, including the visitor IP address described above.

Children

The app has no account or advertising. Optional downloads use the network and are subject to the hosting-log practices described above.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
