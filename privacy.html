Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Booktionary has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, or in-app tracking. English definitions are included in the app; French and Spanish definitions and optional translation packs can be downloaded when you choose them.

Camera

The app needs camera access to read words on the page. Camera frames are processed on your device to recognise text. The app does not upload camera images or the words you look up.

Network

The app contacts booktionary.io to load the language-pack catalog. If you tap Get for a language or translation, it downloads the selected pack. An internet connection is required for the catalog and downloads. After installation, dictionary lookups and translations run on the device and work offline.

Hosting logs

The catalog and pack files are served by GitHub Pages. GitHub says Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security. GitHub's Pages documentation does not specify how long those IP logs are retained. Booktionary does not receive your lookup words or camera images.

Settings

Your language choices and downloaded packs are stored on your device. They are not sent to Booktionary.

Data sharing

Booktionary does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. The hosting provider receives the network information needed to serve catalog and pack requests, including the visitor IP address described above.

Children

The app has no account or advertising. Optional downloads use the network and are subject to the hosting-log practices described above.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the app's data practices change.
